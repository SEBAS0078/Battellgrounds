How Points Work

Every monthly event awards points two ways: how many of your floor’s residents show up, and how your floor places.

🙋

Participation

Each floor earns points based on the percentage of its residents who attend. The bigger your turnout, the bigger your score. Bring your floormates!

🏅

Competition Results

Events with clear rankings award bonus points by placement:

1st

200

2nd

100

3rd

60

4th

40

Good to know

  • If floors tie for a placement, that placement’s points are split evenly between them.
  • Some events don’t have clear placements (participation-based activities, creative submissions, or collaborative events). Those are scored at the organizers’ discretion.
